    Three planes, cross-referenced

    The sagittal, coronal and axial proton-density fat-saturated stacks, side by side. Click or drag in any pane to move the cursor; the other two jump to the nearest slice. Lines show where the other two slices cut through each image. Approximate: these are three separate acquisitions, positioned from each series' stored geometry. The knee can shift a little between scans, so the lines may be off by a few millimetres.

    The stack in 3D

    A projection through one whole stack, computed live in the browser. Drag to rotate, wheel or pinch to zoom. It is a way to see how the slices sit in space, not a diagnostic rendering: slices are 3 mm thick, so the volume is coarse along the stacking direction.
